# Home page brief

## Scope & visitor mode

Persuade + Operate: a professional trader evaluating a trading-intelligence platform
must, within one viewport, see the product analyzing a real market and understand the
full pipeline — data → deterministic analytics → AI interpretation → strategy build
→ backtest → risk-gated MT5 execution. Free exploration; proof over claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: one terminal that proves the whole pipeline from live market data to risk-gated
MT5 execution, refusing the fake-live dashboard (synthetic ticks, invented LATENCIES,
LIVE labels, canned AI chat) that category home pages always ship.
OWN-WORLD: midnight editorial "Institutional Command" — #07080A ground, graphite card
surfaces, hairline borders #23272E, gold #C9A45C used for judgment not decoration,
semantic emerald/red/sky; DM Serif Display editorial headlines, Inter interface, Geist
Mono tabular numerics; one radius system; no orbs, no glow, no glass, no text gradients.
STORY: visitor believes market analysis here is real provider candles processed by
deterministic engines, that AI interprets (never fabricates) that data, and that no
order reaches a broker without the risk engine, the gateway running, and the terminal
connected; they act by scanning, building, backtesting, and executing.
FIRST VIEWPORT: split hero on the 1280px grid — left, editorial DM Serif headline,
two-line promise, and primary Get Started / Explore actions; right, a real-data
AlgoVault terminal showing live XAUUSD candles from /api/analytics/ohlc with computed
regime, structure, VWAP, session, volume, and score rows it labels "provider data";
signature interaction: hovering the terminal swaps between computed-detail tabs.
FORM: code-led; user-brief-pinned institutional-command direction; product terminal
structure leads the build, backed by lifecycle pipeline, backtest console, risk
engine, gateway architecture, and marketplace.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review,
the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
